# Axiomancer Combat
## Heart / Body / Mind Mechanics
This is a rock-paper-scissors, fundamentally. The player chooses between Heart/Body/Mind, this is called an "Argument Type", and will gain certain advantages or disadvantages dependent upon the opponent's selection. Furthermore, the player's choice will dictate which stat is used to determine which stat is used to determine damage and which special attacks they may use. Each special attack has an argument type tied to it. See more in "Special Attacks".

> "Body" > "Mind" > "Heart" > "Body"

Combat itself is fundamentally an "argument" with another individual. The player will "build a case" against the opponents argument using their Body, Mind, and Heart. Whether they build an emotional based argument, or they twist and turn in complex fallacies to confuse the individual and weaken their argument. Hit points represent a person's ability to formulate an argument. At 0 hit-points, they are no longer able to make their case. They are officially either confused, worn down, or they feel too guilty, but no matter the reason, it has the same out-come. They go back to the nearest unlocked rest node, are left with half their HP, full MP, and must re-unlock and re-conquer each map node to return to the argument they lost.

### Benefits for each "Argument Type"
- Body
  - **Attack**: The simplest form of attack. Attack the target with a straight forward argument. Body/Attack is defended against using "Physical Defense"
  - **Special Attack**: Use a skill that falls under the "Body" category. (ie. As Hominim fallacy is not intelligent, it just insults the enemy to weaken their "case")
  - **Defend**: Stand your ground with a "body-based" argument. This will increase your physical defense by 2x unless otherwise notes in a passive skill. However, this will lower their Ailment Defense by 1/2. If the opponent used Attack, they recieve damage equal to 1/4 the player's Physical Damage as Reflect Damage.

- Mind
  - **Attack**: Attack the target's argument using twists and turns to confuse the target and weaken their case. The player only does damage equal to 3/4 of their Mind Attack but grants them a buff next turn where they deal (x) extra damage that does fixed damage regardless of defense. The initial damage is also lessened dependent upon the opponents Mind Defense. The buff only lasts 1 turn. "x" is equal to 1/4 their mind attack, it cannot be evaded or blocked.
  - **Special Attack**: Use a skill that falls under the "Mind" category (ie. Alternative Truth Fallacy, confuse your opponent by confidently citing misinformation. Then deny the truth when faced with push back.)- Disadvantage: Alternatively, a combatant with disadvantage 
  - **Defend**: Stand your ground with "mind-based" argument, Increase your Mind Defense x2 but decrease your Body Defense by 1/2. If the opponent used "Attack", counter their argument by recieving a temporary buff (3 turns), where the player recieves a bonus to their mind attack equal to 1/2 the attack from the opponent (based on the argument type). (ie. Opponent uses Mind Attack, player uses Mind Defend. No Advantage/Disadvantage opponent deals x1/2 damage because player recieves 2x Mind Defense from Defend. Then, player recieves a bonus to Mind Attack equal to x1/4 of the opponents Mind Attack for 3 turns.)

- Heart
  - **Attack**: Heart is tied to a player's Ailment attack. If a player chooses Heart/Attack, they deal damage equal to 1/2 their Ailment Attack. That damage is lowered dependent upon an opponent's Ailment Defense. If the opponent recieves even 1 damage from ailment damage, they recieve a debuff for 3 turns. The debuff has a value attached to it equal to 1/2 the damage they recieved from ailment attack, rounded up. This debuff damages the opponent if they use the "Attack" command again, equal to the value attached to the debuff minus their Ailment defense.
  - **Special Attack**: Choose a skill that falls under the category of Heart. (ie. The Romantic Fallacy where the player will appeal to the opponent with their emotions, saying things like "I feel it so it must be true" which is very difficult to argue against.) These attacks tend to not do any direct damage, but tends to cause debuffs like confuse, slow (where they'll have to make their decisions quicker or they'll be made ofr them), poisoned (where the main arguments are no longer helpful but rather hurt their arguments, thud damaging themselves), bleed (where the opponent can't help but get too emotional and unable to formulate a real argument, thus damaging themseleves)
  - **Defense**: Stand your ground with an "emotional" defense. Ailment Defense is x2 for the turn, however Mind Defense is x1/2. Furthermore, the player will be granted a buff for 3 turns where they will be aware of the enemy's Attack action. Argument Type choice will still be unknown.

### What are the combat actions?
Each selection has different trade-offs dependent upon the "Argument Type" selected.
 - Attack: Use the selected argument type to attack their case.
 - Special Attack: Use a skill that is part of a specific Argument type
 - Defend: Using the specific Argument Type, defend your case
 - Flee: This is the only action that doesn't depend on the Argument type. This is solely to run away and fight this case another day.

### Specifics:

- Advantage: When any combatant has advantage, they gain extra benefits in battle. Here is the extensive list of advantages. (Note: Combatant recieving damage still lowers damage taken base on relevant defensel)

    - Body Attack with advantage: Deal 1.5x damage
    - Mind Attack with advantage: Deal the full 1x damage and their buff that deals extra fixed damage the next turn is 1/2 of the combatant's total Mind Attack.ew
    - Heart Attack with advantage: Deal 3/4 of your total Ailment Attack instead of 1/2. Also, the debuff is 100% of the damage done, not 1/2. However, this debuff has a 15% chance each turn to disappear.

    - Body Special Attack: Each Skill/Fallacy has their own mention of combat advantage.
    - Mind Special Attack: Same
    - Heart Special Attack: Same

> Note: To avoid confusion, Defend ALWAYS sets "argument type"-defense to x2 it's original value and the "argument type weak value"-defense to x1/2. However, there are some special effects sometimes:

    - Body Defend vs Attack: The player receives a bonus where the attacker recieves 1/2 (instead of 1/4) of the damage they did to the player from "Attack"
    - Mind Defend vs Attack: The player recieves a bonus to Mind Attack equal to x1/2 (instead of 1/4) of the opponents Mind Attack for 3 turns
    - Heart Defend vs Attack: The player will be granted a buff for 3 turns where they will be aware of the enemy's Attack action and Argument type. (instead of just attack action)

> Note Defend vs. Defend has a special interaction
    - Defend vs. Defend (regardless of Argument) - **Normal Creature**: Will increment the "Agree to Disagree" counter by 1. For most encounters, if you get that to 3, the two of you agree to disagree and you learn a piece of information regarding the lore within Axiomancer.
    - Defend vs Defend (regardless of Argument) - **Elite Creature**: Elite Creatures have a max "Agree to Disagree" counter of 5. However, Agree to Disagree with an Elite provides rare items that are otherwise impossible to get by other means.
    - Defend vs Defend (regardless of Argument - **Boss Creature**): To obtain the Agree-to-Disagree victory from a boss, you must obtain 10 Agree to Disagree counters. However, considering the difficulty of triggering this scenario, the player is rewarded with various unique rewards. They'll obtain a scroll to summon the boss up to 3 times in combat, unique items only acquirable through triggering this event. Lastly, the player has the possibility of acquiring a quest item that, when enough gathered, can be traded in (in the final area) for the best items in the game.

## Effects during battle

There are buffs and debuffs and they're not that complicated but can turn the tide of combat very quickly.

- Buffs/Debuffs: The icon(s) will be underneath your HP/MP bar and the combatant's HP/MP bar. Buffs under the player with the buff and Debuffs under the player's bar who has the debuff. Debuffs will be marked with a Red border whereas Buffs will be marked with a Green border.

- Examples:
    - Player uses Mind Attack, they now have a buff until the end of the next turn where their next attack triggers a separate attack that causes fixed damage. In this case, this is a BUFF so it'll be under the player's HP/MP bar
    - Player uses Heart Attack and triggers a special debuff. The player deals their initial 1/2 of their Heart Attack damage minus the combatant's Ailment Defense. The combatant recieving that damage also recieves a DEBUFF for 3 turns where they take (x) damage (Minus Ailment Defense) every time they use "Attack". So in this case, the combatant recieving damage will show a DEBUFF under their name, dictated with a Red border.
    - Player uses Body Defend. They take some sort of damagem but will then recieve a BUFF called "Reflect" where whenever the player is "Attacked" or recieved damage from a Special Attack, the attacking combatant takes (x) damaage equal to the Reflect Value. So the icon for "Reflect" will render under the player with a Green border to show "Buff".
