## Childhood
- Player is about 10 years old
- Start out in a small fishing town being raised by your father
- Befriend someone of the same age and opposite gender
- Fast-forward 3 years of spending most of your time with your new friend and acquiring feelings for your friend
- Friend moves away
- Leave with a cart of fish and their favorite horse with a plan to build a boat and visit your friend
- Enters forest, gathers wood
- Enters cave, gathers iron ore
- Enters major city, sells fish to refine ore and build the boat
- While in city, player hears rumor that the most wealthy and valued city, which is surrounded by a labyrinth the size of Russia,is looking for someone to become the King's new advisor and that the labyrinth is now open
- Player uses boat to float down river
- Player visits multiple islands, while on island player witnesses ritual where the island nominates another 13 year old boy to face the labyrinth and become the King's new advisor
- Player arrives at best friend's village and again, witnesses
- Player returns home
- Player decides to leave home again to become the King's advisor
