# Axiomancer Status Effects Documentation

## Overview

Status effects in Axiomancer represent the psychological and philosophical horror that permeates every aspect of existence in this dying world. Each effect is designed to be psychologically devastating, reflecting the despairing theme of inevitable human frailty and the crushing weight of philosophical torment.

These effects occur naturally through combat mechanics, fallacy usage, and environmental interactions, creating a complex web of mental and emotional consequences that make every philosophical confrontation a heart-wrenching experience.

## Categories

### 1. Natural Combat Effects
Status effects that occur automatically through basic combat mechanics, without requiring skills or spells.

### 2. Buffs - Hope in a Hopeless World
Temporary advantages that provide fleeting moments of power, but always at a terrible psychological cost.

### 3. Debuffs - The Crushing Weight of Despair
Devastating psychological effects that represent the fundamental brokenness of the human condition.

## Implementation Notes

### Bleak & Heart-Wrenching Design Philosophy
Every status effect in Axiomancer is designed to be psychologically devastating, reflecting the despairing philosophical horror theme. The descriptions focus on:
- **Emotional Manipulation**: Effects that destroy personal worth and self-trust
- **Social Isolation**: Effects that sever human connections and create alienation
- **Reality Fragmentation**: Effects that question the nature of existence itself
- **Moral Corruption**: Effects that erode ethical foundations and create hypocrisy
- **Existential Despair**: Effects that reveal the fundamental meaninglessness of existence

### Combat Integration
Status effects occur through:
1. **Natural Combat Mechanics**: Basic attacks, defenses automatically apply effects
2. **Skill Usage**: Fallacies and abilities apply specific status effects
3. **Environmental Interactions**: Map nodes and events can apply status effects
4. **Equipment Effects**: Items can modify or apply status effects

### Visual System
- **Green Border**: Buffs (hope in a hopeless world)
- **Red Border**: Debuffs (crushing weight of despair)
- **Icons**: Each effect has a unique emoji identifier
- **Duration Tracking**: Effects show remaining turns
- **Stacking**: Some effects can stack for increased potency

This comprehensive status effects system transforms philosophical combat into a truly horrifying psychological experience where every effect represents a different facet of human despair and existential torment.
